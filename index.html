Getting Started

How to use Mood Orb

01 Open the app. The orb will begin its gentle idle pulse automatically. On your very first launch, a silent hand animation demonstrates the interaction.
02 Press and hold the orb to inhale. Feel it expand slowly beneath your touch.
03 Release to exhale. The orb contracts back to rest.
04 Stay as long as you need. The orb's color shifts over time — cool blue into teal, then warm gold, deepening into violet the longer your session continues.

FAQ

Common questions

Does Mood Orb require an account?

No. Mood Orb requires nothing from you — no account, no email, no sign-in of any kind. Open it and breathe.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Mood Orb has no internet dependency whatsoever. It works on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere.

Will it send me notifications?

Never. Mood Orb will not ask for notification permissions and will never interrupt your day.

The orb doesn't appear centered on my screen.

Try force-closing the app and reopening it. If the issue persists, please contact us below with your iPhone model and iOS version.

The ghost hand animation plays every time I open the app.

The intro animation should only play once on first launch. If it keeps reappearing, try deleting and reinstalling the app. Your experience will not be affected as there is no data to lose.

Is there a breathing guide or timer I can follow?

Mood Orb is intentionally guideless. The pace is entirely yours — there is no right or wrong rhythm. Let the orb follow your breath, not the other way around.
